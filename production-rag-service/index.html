AtlasRAG
Evidence Retrieval Platform.

A citation-first document retrieval service. It ingests text, stores chunks in SQLite, ranks evidence with BM25, and returns extractive answers with source citations.

ENGINEERING NOTES

Grounding over guesswork

Includes document upsert, listing and deletion, Persian and English token support, citation metadata, explicit no-evidence responses, and retrieval evaluation using Hit Rate@k and Mean Reciprocal Rank.

This implementation is retrieval-focused: it does not claim to generate answers with an LLM, and its sample corpus is illustrative rather than a real-world benchmark.
