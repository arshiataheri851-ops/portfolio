## 4. Delivery method

ERYNDEX uses the following four-stage process:

1. **Understand** — clarify the business need, users, constraints, existing systems, and desired outcome.
2. **Scope** — document deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, milestones, responsibilities, dependencies, and acceptance criteria.
3. **Build** — implement in reviewable increments, communicate progress, and validate against agreed criteria.
4. **Handover** — provide the agreed code, technical documentation, and knowledge transfer. Maintenance and ongoing support are separately scoped.

Before a project begins, the parties should agree on the estimate, timeline, payment terms, ownership/licensing, acceptance process, change control, confidentiality, and any support expectations.
